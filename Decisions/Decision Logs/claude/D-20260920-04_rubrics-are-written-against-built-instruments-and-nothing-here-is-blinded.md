## D-20260920-04 — Rubrics are written against built instruments, and nothing here is blinded

**Her rulings, verbatim (2026-09-20, mobile):** *"A rubric shouldn't be frozen before eval design...
Its effective design requires the instrument already be built, not even just designed but rubric
design is done against the built evaluation instrument."* and *"Also there is no need to get so
serious and freezing stuff etc. Everything is tracked in git. I am able to reach everything you make
if I want to so stop worrying so much about "blinding" and other clean lan fantasy concerns"*

**Action:** Move the B01 key from the private workbench to this repo, beside its instrument, and
retire the private `baseline keys` folder; strip "held outside this repo", "frozen", and "so a
re-take stays honest" from every live sentence in this repo; correct the build order this seat had
proposed.

**Method:** File moves and in-place wording corrections by this seat; the instrument's footer and the
key's header changed one line each, which is below the version-up threshold. DEFAULT tooling.

**Repetition:** 3 (D-01 through D-03 share the Method). New rationale: a correction of order and of
posture, at her ruling, not new design.

**The corrected order, for the record:** standards → evidence plan → the instrument is **built**
(baseline, exit ticket, performance task) → its key or rubric is written **against the built
instrument** → the lesson that leads to it. The playbook's design-time layer still exists, but a
rigor rubric for lesson quality is written against a built exemplar lesson, and a scoring rubric is
written against the built ticket. Nothing is written against a plan. This is also the Paper C
position: criterion text is counts and ratios over units the artifact defines, so the artifact has to
exist first.

**Alternatives rejected:**
- **Keep the key private on habit.** Rejected: its location was justified only by a blinding rationale
  she has struck; everything is in git and reachable by her regardless, and a key beside its
  instrument is where a rubric written against that instrument belongs.
- **Version the instrument to v03 for a one-line footer change.** Rejected: the file-naming rule
  reserves version-up for significant change.

**Rationale:** her rulings, quoted. The seriousness she is striking out was this seat importing
psychometric-lab posture into a one-learner course where the learner owns the repository.

**Falsifier:** a future instrument ships with its key written before the instrument exists, or a
lesson ships with a rubric written against its plan rather than against the built ticket.
